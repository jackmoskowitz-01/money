## Reddit Distribution Plan

### Target Subreddit
**r/CommercialRealEstate** (~60k members, active, mix of brokers/investors/developers)

### Posts to Share

| Blog Post | Reddit Title | Approach |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| Cold Email Templates | "I compiled 6 cold email templates for tenant rep prospecting — sharing them here" | Lead with value. Paste 2-3 templates directly in the post. Link to full set at the bottom. Don't be salesy. |
| Leaving Deals on the Table | "What's holding most tenant rep brokers back from scaling?" | Frame as a discussion, not a promo. Share 2-3 insights from the post. Ask the community what they think. |
| Prospecting Playbook | "Built a prospecting playbook for tenant rep — feedback welcome" | Position as sharing your work, asking for feedback. CRE Reddit respects humility. |
| Lease Expiration Strategy | "How are you all approaching lease expiration prospecting?" | Question-first. Share your strategy in the body. Feels like a peer conversation, not marketing. |
| AI in CRE Prospecting | "Anyone using AI for CRE prospecting? Here's what's actually working." | Timely topic. Share real results. Avoid hype. Reddit hates AI hype. |
| Automation + Personal Touch | Don't post this one directly — too close to a sales pitch. | Instead, comment on related threads with insights from this post. Build authority in comments. |

### Reddit Rules of Engagement
- **Never link-drop.** Always provide standalone value in the post itself.
- **Engage in comments** for at least 48 hours after posting. Answer every question.
- **Comment on other threads** in the subreddit regularly — not just when you post. Build a reputation first.
- **Cadence:** 1 Reddit post every 2-3 weeks max. Too frequent = spam.
- **Flair:** Use appropriate post flair. "Discussion" works for most of these.
- **Also lurk in:** r/CRE, r/realestate (less targeted but larger), r/sales (for prospecting/cold email posts)

## Email List Building Strategy

### Lead Magnet
Gate the full cold email templates behind email capture. The blog post gives 2-3 templates. The download gives all 6 + subject line variants + follow-up sequences.

### Capture Points
1. **Blog post CTA** — Inline CTA within the cold email templates blog post: "Want all 6 templates + follow-up sequences? Download the full pack."
2. **Popup/slide-in** — Trigger on the cold email templates blog after 30 seconds or 50% scroll.
3. **LinkedIn bio link** — Link to the templates landing page from the company LinkedIn page and founder's profile.
4. **Reddit** — When sharing templates on Reddit, mention "I have a full pack with follow-ups if anyone wants it — drop a comment or DM." Then follow up with the landing page link via DM.
5. **Exit intent** — On all blog posts, show exit-intent popup offering the templates.

### Nurture Sequence (Post-Capture)
1. **Email 1 (immediate):** Deliver the templates. Introduce the company. (See email-capture-copy.md for full copy.)
2. **Email 2 (Day 3):** "How to customize these templates for your market" — quick tips + link to the Prospecting Playbook blog.
3. **Email 3 (Day 7):** "The lease expiration angle most brokers miss" — link to Lease Expiration blog.
4. **Email 4 (Day 14):** "What if you could send 10x the outreach without hiring a VA?" — soft intro to the product.
5. **Email 5 (Day 21):** "See it in action" — product demo CTA. Direct ask.

### Success Metrics
- Email capture rate target: 8-12% of blog visitors on the templates post
- Email open rate target: 45%+ (niche B2B audience should be high)
- Demo request rate from nurture: 3-5% of list
